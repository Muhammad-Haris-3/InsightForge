Full formal requirements are in **InsightForge_SRS_v1.0.docx** (uploaded separately to this Project) — covers feasibility study, SDLC methodology (iterative/incremental, solo-adapted), functional/non-functional requirements, architecture with rejected alternatives, conceptual DB design, milestone plan (M0-M7), and risk analysis.
